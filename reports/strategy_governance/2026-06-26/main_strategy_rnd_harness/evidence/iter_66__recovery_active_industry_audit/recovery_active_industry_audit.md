## Plain Conclusion

- I66 uses `normalized_active_weight`, meaning strategy industry composition after normalizing by live exposure minus CSI300 industry weight.
- Fold2 recovery is internally overweight 白酒/电气设备/银行 and underweight 软件服务/证券/半导体 versus CSI300. The benchmark recovery window itself is negative, so adding recovery exposure did not help.
- Fold4 recovery is internally overweight 白酒/银行 and underweight 证券/半导体/软件服务. The benchmark recovery window is also negative, so the industry tilt cannot rescue the trade.
- Fold5 recovery is internally overweight 银行 during a clearly positive recovery window. This is the one case where recovery participation and industry leadership align.
- The evidence supports a new design direction: recovery participation should not only ask whether the index is recovering. It should first decide whether the recovery window is broadly tradable, then decide whether to stay benchmark-like or permit industry tilt.
- Raw active weights remain mostly negative because live exposure is below 100%. The normalized active view is the right lens for judging the composition of the capital that the strategy actually deployed.
